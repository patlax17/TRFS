Our Story

Wellness That Fits Your Life

At Total Range, I believe that better movement leads to a better quality of life.

Our Approach

Founded with a passion for helping people feel their best, Total Range specializes in personalized assisted stretching designed to improve flexibility, increase mobility, and help clients move with greater comfort and confidence. Whether you're an athlete looking to enhance performance, a busy professional dealing with daily stiffness, or someone simply wanting to feel better in your body, our goal is to help you reach your full movement potential.

As a Certified Fascia Stretch Practitioner, I take a client-focused approach to every session. No two bodies are the same, which is why each stretch is tailored to your individual needs, limitations, and goals. I take the time to understand how your body moves so we can create a safe, effective experience that supports long-term mobility and wellness.

At Total Range, you're more than just an appointment. You're a partner in the process. My mission is to provide a welcoming, professional environment where clients feel supported, heard, and empowered to move better every day.

Whether you're visiting my home-based studio or booking a mobile session, you can expect personalized care, attention to detail, and a commitment to helping you achieve lasting results.

Let's work together to improve your flexibility, increase your range of motion, and help you move through life with greater ease.
